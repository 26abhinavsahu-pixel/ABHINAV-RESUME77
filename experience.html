// 02 — PRACTICAL IMMERSION

Learning business from the ground up.

First-hand observation, customer communication, and commercial operational exposure in a dynamic family trading business.

Marketing Trainee 2–3 Months Duration Babina Cantt, Jhansi

Shankar Traders (Family Business)

SECTOR CLASSIFICATION Commercial Trading & Retail

During this 2 to 3-month immersion, I worked on the frontlines of our family enterprise, gaining indispensable practical exposure to everyday commerce. Instead of relying solely on theoretical business models, this experience gave me a grounded understanding of how genuine customer relationships, pricing nuances, and operational workflows drive sustained local trade.

Primary Responsibilities & Learnings

  • Customer Interaction & Requirement Assessment: Interacted directly with customers on a daily basis, learning about their specific requirements, expectations, and buying behavior across various price segments.
  • Product Catalog & Dynamic Pricing: Mastered extensive product information and understood the commercial logic behind pricing in a day-to-day trading environment, including cost margins and competitive trade factors.
  • Sales & Marketing Operations: Observed real-time sales transactions, customer retention tactics, marketing activities, and basic end-to-end business operations that sustain a commercial enterprise.
CORE TAKEAWAY

Understanding how trust is built with individual buyers provides a crucial anchor for studying large-scale marketing and digital analytics at IIM Bangalore.

// LEARNING PILLARS

The Anatomy of Retail Commerce

Breaking down the three practical disciplines observed and practiced during my time at Shankar Traders.

[ 01 ] INTERACTION

Customer Empathy

Direct, unfiltered dialogue with diverse patrons. Learned to listen actively to buyer queries, identify unspoken needs, and communicate product benefits clearly without jargon.

Observation:

Customer loyalty stems from consistent transparency and responsive service rather than aggressive pitching.

[ 02 ] ECONOMICS

Product & Pricing

Catalog familiarity across inventory lines. Studied how wholesale acquisition costs translate to customer-facing retail prices while factoring in volume, margins, and seasonal demand.

Observation:

Pricing flexibility and perceived value determine transaction velocity in daily trading cycles.

[ 03 ] OPERATIONS

Operational Flow

Examining the day-to-day rhythm of store administration, stock handling, cash-flow discipline, and the coordination necessary to keep a family business running efficiently.

Observation:

Operational stability is the backbone of commercial success; without it, marketing cannot deliver sustained results.

// THE ENTREPRENEURIAL BRIDGE

Connecting retail reality to digital scaling.

My stint at Shankar Traders revealed that every sophisticated digital product or e-commerce platform ultimately solves the same human problems: finding the right product, trusting the pricing, and enjoying a seamless fulfillment experience.

At IIM Bangalore, I apply these grounded lessons to digital business models — exploring how modern web interfaces, predictive analytics, and automated CRM systems can enhance and scale traditional commerce principles.

From Shop Floor to Digital Strategy

  • 01
    Customer Requirements → User Persona Mapping

    Translating customer conversations into structured target audience segments and digital user journeys.

  • 02
    Trading Margins → Unit Economics & Pricing Models

    Applying real product costs and margin realities to spreadsheet models and financial forecasts.

  • 03
    Local Store Operations → Scalable Startup Architectures

    Understanding inventory friction and fulfillment bottlenecks to design streamlined digital solutions.
